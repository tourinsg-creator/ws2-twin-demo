Wafer geometry tools in a 300 mm bay

Several double-sided wafer geometry tools share an overhead-transport loop and a stocker. Each tool is modelled station by station: two load ports, an edge-grip robot, a pre-aligner with a thickness probe, and the measurement chamber. All dimensions and station times are generic placeholders, set so one tool measures a wafer about once a minute.

Model and placeholder values

  • Tool model: two load ports; edge-grip robot; pre-aligner with thickness probe; measurement chamber where both sides are acquired at once; repeats keep the wafer in the chamber. Light tower states map to the SEMI E10 states shown.
  • Footprint (placeholder): tool 1.7 × 2.5 m plus 0.5 m load ports; 1.0 m service clearance on every side, shared between neighbouring tools; 3.0 m aisle.
  • Placeholder station times (s): dock + map 35, undock 20, robot pick 6, align + thickness 14, robot to pallet 5, pallet in 12, acquire 25 (Standard 40) × repeats, pallet out 10, robot back to slot 8. That gives a 60 s pallet cycle.
  • Placeholder events: handler/gauge alarm MTBF 36 h, 15 min response + 20–60 min repair + 5 min warm-up; PM every 7 days for 4 h + 30 min thermal settle; 10 min monitor wafer every 24 h (adjustable); recalibration 60 min if |Δ thickness| > 30 nm.
  • AMHS: stocker at the bay end, OHT 2 m/s, 12 s pick and 12 s drop. Lots arrive at random (Poisson) at the set demand.
  • Repeatability σ 8 nm for thickness and the tool offsets are placeholders. The same seed gives the same run after Reset.
